## Rules
- The design space is 1080x1920. Every SVG's viewBox is in that same scale, so an asset drawn at its native viewBox size fits the scene.
- Outlines are 8px `ink`, with round joins and caps.
- Shadow: one per item, `ink` at 20% opacity. Flat top-view items get the same shape shifted down. Standing items get an ellipse on the ground.
- Character layers (`character-body`, `-eyes-*`, `-mouth-*`) all share the 600x700 viewBox. Stack them in the order body, then eyes, then mouth.
- Oven layers (`oven-inside`, then the pizza, then `oven-closed`) share the 700x800 viewBox. The window is a real transparent hole at x 150–550, y 320–610 (corner radius 30). Centre the pizza at about (350, 480) in oven coordinates, with a diameter of about 300–330.
- `hand-hint`: the fingertip is near the top-left of the image, at about (53, 23) in its 220x280 viewBox. The hand is tilted 12°.
- Topping and cheese images are top-down views, because they sit on the pizza.

The assets are generated by a script (kept outside this folder). `images-v1/` holds the first version.

## Landscape layout

The game is now landscape. The new background is `images/bg-kitchen-landscape.svg`, 1920x1080 (16:9). `bg-kitchen.svg` stays the portrait background.

### World and scaling
- The world is always **1080 units high**. Its width follows the screen: `W = 1080 × aspect`. That gives W = 1440 at 4:3, 1920 at 16:9 and 2400 at 20:9.
- **Background:** scale it by `k = max(W / 1920, 1)` to cover the screen. Anchor it **bottom-centre**, so the counter is never cropped.
  - Wide phones (20:9) lose the top of the background: 216 background units at 20:9.
  - Tablets (4:3) lose 240 units on each side.
- **Background safe area** (always visible from 4:3 to 20:9, in background coordinates): **x 240–1680, y 216–1080**. Only decoration lies outside it: corner cabinets, bunting, jar tops and the top of the window. The counter surface is plain across the full width.
- **Zones:** x positions are fractions of W, so they line up with the background at every ratio. y positions are absolute.
- **Asset scale:** draw every gameplay asset at **0.75×** its native viewBox. The asset outline is 8px, so it renders at 6px, which matches the background, drawn with a 6px outline at 1:1. Don't mix scales between objects, or the outlines stop matching.

- **No interactive elements:** the bottom 8% (**y > 994**) and 4% on each side (**x < 0.04·W and x > 0.96·W**, which is 77px at 16:9). Art may extend into these strips, but hit areas may not.
